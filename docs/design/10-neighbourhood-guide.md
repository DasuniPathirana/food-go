# Neighbourhood guide refresh

The user asked for a more pleasant, creative, researched interface and confirmed the audience is **all**. Preserve the light theme, clear dark lettering, highly interactive approved map, game loop, social walls and optional avatars. This is an authorised UI refresh within those product constraints.

## Grounded systems considered

1. Food hall signage: direct names and vivid food cues.
2. Expedition game HUD: compact movement and progress controls.
3. Social restaurant diary: personal stories beside places.
4. Travel passport: collection and milestones as a growing record.
5. Market menu board: appetite, useful choices and clear pricing hierarchy.
6. Community noticeboard: people and local discoveries.
7. Fold-out neighbourhood guide: one open map, a clear masthead and destination-specific sections.

These cover public signage, interface traditions and printed guides. The direction seed `ea063235` assigned the seventh grounded structure. The style tool's external material challengers were assessed against audience identification and task clarity: layered paper cut, indoor amber installation, spectral instrument, hard-shadow brutalism and parametric identity were declined because their literal navigation/materials would weaken a familiar food discovery task. Retained disciplines are a committed palette, explicit hierarchy, compact controls and a recognisable mark. The existing light map/food brief takes precedence over dark or theatrical challenger materials. The implementation is code-led, with existing food photography and the approved scene as visual evidence.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Food Go feels like a local guide people use together. Each destination has its own useful composition; a repeated dashboard-card template does not organise the product.

**OWN-WORLD:** Fresh white, dark ink, tomato actions and butter-yellow progression. Bricolage Grotesque supplies concise characterful headings; Source Sans 3 carries reading. Open dividers and food photographs take priority over nested cream containers. Collectible marks belong to Passport and food trails.

**STORY:** Find somewhere on Explore, bring the experience to Feed, collect it in FoodDex/Passport, and follow another trail. Game rewards and character customisation remain optional and findable.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A horizontal desktop masthead replaces the large sidebar and daily-promotion card. The map opens wide below compact navigation/search. On phones, Explore stays centred in the bottom navigation. Feed shows real story content sooner, with an economical composer and inline wall controls.

**FORM:** Fold-out neighbourhood guide, seventh grounded candidate, seed `ea063235`. The signature is a map-first guide joined to a clear, collected-food Passport rather than repeated promotional headers.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

No new shipping raster is required: existing locally credited food photographs remain the visual content, the approved map and avatar graphics remain intact, and typography is self-hosted with its source licences.
