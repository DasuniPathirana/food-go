Melbourne map sources

Satellite

Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2025. The true-colour Sentinel-2B observation was captured on 5 March 2025. Food Go cropped the 10 metre Level-2A product to the city centre, reprojected it to Web Mercator and encoded the extract as WebP.

Source observation, via Element 84 Earth Search · Copernicus Sentinel data legal notice

Aerial photography

City of Melbourne, May 2020. The original true orthophoto was taken from an aircraft at 10 centimetre ground sampling distance. Food Go exported, cropped and downsampled the city-centre image to about one metre per pixel. The source and this extract are available under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.

City aerial dataset · City imagery service · CC BY 4.0

Buildings and places

City of Melbourne, 2020 Building Footprints and Landmarks and Places of Interest. Food Go extracted building components inside the map area, rounded their coordinates and subtracted each structure’s base elevation from the measured component elevations. 3D shows these heights in metres above local ground. Landmark pins use the City’s reference points for the market, shopping centres and Federation Square. Entrances and individual shops may be elsewhere within a site.

Building source · Landmark source · CC BY 4.0

Coverage is central Melbourne. The satellite observation and the building survey have different capture dates.
